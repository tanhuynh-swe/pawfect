# Privacy Policy

**Pawfect Pipeline** - Automated Video Creator for Pet Care Content

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

## 1. Overview

Pawfect Pipeline respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 2. Information We Collect

**API Credentials:**
- TikTok API credentials (Client Key, Client Secret)
- YouTube OAuth tokens and API keys
- Anthropic API keys for AI script generation

**Video Content:**
- Scripts and metadata you create
- Video files generated through the service
- Publishing history and timestamps

**Usage Data:**
- Command logs and processing records
- Error reports and debugging information

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To generate, render, and publish your videos
- To improve the service and fix issues
- To comply with legal obligations

## 4. Data Security

- Credentials are stored locally on your machine, never transmitted to us
- API calls go directly to TikTok and YouTube
- We do not store your video content on external servers
- Treat your `.env` file and credentials as sensitive

## 5. Third-Party Services

This service integrates with:
- **TikTok API** - review their privacy policy
- **YouTube Data API** - review their privacy policy
- **Anthropic API** - review their privacy policy
- **Pexels / Pixabay APIs** - review their privacy policies

You are responsible for understanding their data practices.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access and delete your local data
- Revoke API credentials at any time
- Opt out of service use

## 7. Data Retention

- Local data is retained as long as needed
- Deleting your workspace folder removes all local records
- API credentials can be revoked in platform settings

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy at any time. Changes are effective immediately.
